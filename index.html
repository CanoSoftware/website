About the studio

I'm TJ. I live in Maryland, I work in corporate IT, and I build these apps at night and on weekends.

Each one solved a problem in my own house first. I kept forgetting when I last changed the furnace filter, so I built Steward. I wanted oil changes and tire rotations tracked in one place, so I built Roadworthy. Homestead came from wanting to see the family's week ahead without Sunday night turning into a meal-planning scramble.

Buy them outright if you want to. Two offer a subscription, but every app has a one-time price, and it doesn't expire.

New apps, when there are new apps.

I'll email you when I ship something. That's a few times a year, and I won't use your address for anything else.
