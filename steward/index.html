What it keeps
01

Appliances

Model and serial, warranty end date, what you paid, and every dollar spent on it since — per appliance, in the room it lives in.

02

The schedule

Filters, flushes, gutters, smoke-alarm batteries. Give it an interval once and Steward carries the next date forward every time you mark it done.

03

Household sharing

Invite the people you live with through iCloud. Everyone sees the same house, so "did anyone call the plumber" stops being a question.

04

Vendors & emergency

Every contractor's number in one list, and where the water shutoff is — the two things you need when it's already going wrong.
