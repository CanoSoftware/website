Homestead

Your family's day, on the wall and in your pocket.

Chores, calendar, meals, and groceries, kept in one place your whole family can see and edit.

Coming soon on the App Store See pricing →

Features

Everything the fridge whiteboard was trying to do

One system for chores, calendar, meals, and groceries. No account to set up, nothing tracking you.

Chores

Assigned per person, set to repeat, and easy to check off. No red badges nagging anyone.

Shared calendar

Everyone in the household sees it and can edit it. No more guessing whose day it is.

Meal planning

Plan the week and the grocery list builds itself.

Weather at a glance

Current conditions on the board, the full forecast in your pocket.

Timers

Pasta, homework, screen time. Start it from the kitchen, see it on the board.

Photo slideshow

When the day winds down, the board dims and drifts through family photos.

Built for the wall, not just the pocket

Mount an old iPad in the kitchen and it becomes the household's day at a glance: agenda, chores, and conditions, always on.
